## Owner questions (design, not defects) — merged from both agents

1. **Server `maxQPS`:** per instance or total? Decides #1. (BE-Q1, FE-Q6)
2. **Overload semantics:** should a saturated node forward only what it serves (realistic), forward everything (worst case), or should a toggle choose? Decides #8. (BE-Q2)
3. **Queues:** are the queue's `consumers` the consumers, or are the downstream servers? Today both are counted (#24). (BE-Q3)
4. **Retries:** is `1 + e·r` meant as an upper bound ("up to")? Should overload errors drive retries? (#23) (BE-Q4)
5. **Simulated or removed:** should read/write split, LB strategy and instances be simulated as lessons and docs assume, or removed from the UI? (#1, #7, #16) (FE-Q6)
6. **Analysis model:** auto-analyze on every change (cheap), or keep explicit Analyze with an "out of date" state? (#26, idea #93) (FE-Q1)
7. **Canvas restore:** should the last canvas return silently on reload, or behind a "Restore last session?" prompt? (#5) (BE-Q10)
8. **Phone use:** should the playground stay desktop-only? A read-only share viewer on phones would fix #19. (FE-Q2)
9. **Themes:** fully tokenise Light and Matrix, or ship Dark plus a properly built Light only? (#17, #18, #30) (FE-Q5)
10. **Supabase:** what are the anon RLS policies on `playground_stats`? Does it store IP or UA? On live, the counters did not render: is `Content-Range` exposed, and are the counts working? Should the counters stay public? (#32, #33) (BE-Q5, BE-Q8, FE-Q4)
11. **AdSense:** is the site approved and serving? All slots are unfilled on live. Is "Privacy & messaging" on (it covers most of #29)? Are Auto ads (anchor/vignette) intended over the canvas? (BE-Q7, FE-Q3)
